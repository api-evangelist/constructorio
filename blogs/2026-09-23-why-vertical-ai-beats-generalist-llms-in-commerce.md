One of the most consequential things taking place in commerce today is the revolution in how discovery happens. This is taking three forms: First, where that discovery happens (within retailers’ owned properties; via Answer Engines; or via personal assistant agents like Instinct, Muse, and GrokBot). Second, how the discovery happens (traditional forms of discovery like search; onsite agents; or something else).
